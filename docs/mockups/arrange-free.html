Arrange panels anywhere

One way to arrange, on a phone and on a computer. A mockup of Today with example panels; changes stay on this device only.

Hold any panel (a finger, or a long mouse press) and every panel can be arranged. Drag a panel anywhere: the gap it leaves stays, and panels it lands on move down. Drag a side to change its size. ✕ removes. Done, Esc or a tap on an empty spot finishes.
