Rules I would use (change any)

✓Ticking every subtask does not tick the parent. You tick it yourself.
✓Ticking the parent leaves subtasks as they are.
✓One level of subtasks only (no subtasks of subtasks).
✓Subtasks follow the parent to Today, Done and "Not today". They do not show on Today alone.
✓Claude can add and tick subtasks.
